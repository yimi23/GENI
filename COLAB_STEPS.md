## ⚡ Quick Steps (5 minutes)

### 1️⃣ Upload Notebook (30 seconds)

- Click **"File → Upload notebook"**
- Select: **`TRAIN_GENI_YOLO.ipynb`** (on your Desktop)

### 2️⃣ Enable GPU (30 seconds)

- Click **"Runtime → Change runtime type"**
- Select: **T4 GPU**
- Click **Save**

### 3️⃣ Upload Training Data (1 minute)

- Click **folder icon** (📁 left sidebar)
- Click **upload button** (top of files panel)
- Select: **`geni_training_data.zip`** (22MB, on your Desktop)
- Wait for upload bar to complete

### 4️⃣ Run Training (15 minutes)

- Click **"Runtime → Run all"**
