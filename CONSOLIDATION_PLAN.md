## New Architecture (Voice-Only Vision)

### User Flow: Margaret's Experience

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           🎤 GENI Voice Interface              │
│                                                │
│        [Camera Feed - Pill Cabinet]            │
│                                                │
│     "Margaret, it's time for your BP meds"     │
│                                                │
│            [Tap Microphone to Talk]            │
│                                                │
│  Last Activity:                                │
│  • Sarah replied: "Calling you now"            │
│  • Alert sent to Sarah (2:05 PM)               │
│  • Medication reminder (2:00 PM)               │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**NO TEXT INPUT. VOICE ONLY.**

### Conversation Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: "What should I take today?"
```
Margaret: "GENI, what medications am I supposed to take today?"
[Camera auto-activates, shows pill cabinet]

GENI: "Today is Friday. You have your morning Metformin which you've 
already taken. Your afternoon blood pressure medication is due at 2pm - 
that's in about an hour. I can see your Friday compartment is ready."
```

#### Scenario 2: Missed Medication + Symptoms
```
[Timer fires at 2:00 PM]
GENI: "Margaret, it's time for your afternoon medication."

[Camera checks - pills still there after 5 minutes]
GENI: "I notice you haven't taken your pills yet. How are you feeling?"

Margaret: "I forgot. I'm feeling a bit dizzy."

GENI: "That's okay, these things happen. Dizziness can sometimes come 
with delayed blood pressure medication. Let's get you sorted. I'm going 
to let Sarah know so she can check in on you. In the meantime, try to 
sit down and take your medication when you're ready."

[WhatsApp to Sarah]:
"Hi Sarah, this is GENI. Margaret hasn't taken her 2pm blood pressure 
medication yet and mentioned she's feeling dizzy. She's aware and I've 
reminded her to sit down. Might be worth a quick check-in. No immediate 
emergency but wanted you to know."

[Sarah replies: "Thanks GENI, calling her now"]

GENI: "Good news, Margaret. Sarah got my message and she's calling you now."
```

- [ ] **Alert Types:**
  - [ ] `low` urgency - "casual check-in"
  - [ ] `medium` urgency - "worth checking in"
  - [ ] `high` urgency - "needs attention now"

- [ ] **Replace Generic Emergency Alerts**
  - [ ] Update `routes/emergency.ts` to use new service
  - [ ] Remove "🚨 EMERGENCY ALERT" hardcoded messages
  - [ ] Generate contextual messages instead

### Phase 4: Auto-Speak Incoming (1 hour)

  - [ ] Extract sender name + message text
